\section{Conclusion}

This paper studies risk-conditioned execution governance for windy
suspended-payload UAV transport. The proposed governor leaves the planner,
payload MPC, and SO(3) controller unchanged while adapting execution
aggressiveness through speed scale and
acceleration scale. The protocol-split evaluation shows strong
specialists: Wind-Level 0.85 leads the single-goal mission protocol
and Fixed Scale 0.80 leads the goal-reissue stress protocol. Across the
two tested protocols, Risk Adapter v1 provides the strongest
balanced learned / risk-conditioned result in the evaluated set by mean valid
count, worst-protocol count, and total regret. Failure-group and
representative-trace analyses further show
why aggregate success alone is insufficient. The current evidence supports a
bounded balanced-robustness claim under the tested protocols, without claiming
statistical significance, certified safety, learned uniform domination, or a
new final method variant.
